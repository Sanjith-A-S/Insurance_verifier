01 · Problem statement

Why is claim investigation difficult?

A motor-insurance claim is not a single document. It is a collection of evidence that must be understood together.

THE CORE PROBLEM
Important claim information is scattered across different formats and sources.

Investigators need to connect what the claimant says, what the policy covers, what photographs show and what the repair estimate records. Reviewing these separately can make it difficult to form a complete and consistent picture.

PEOPLE AFFECTED

Claimants

May be unsure which documents or photographs are needed and what action comes next.

Verifiers

Must review evidence, identify gaps, request clarification and record a reasoned outcome.

02 · Existing difficulties

Where does the current process become challenging?

Common workflow challenges the platform is designed to address—not a claim that every insurer follows the same process.

01 / FRAGMENTED INPUTS

Evidence in many places

Policy PDFs, claim narratives, images and estimates may need separate review.

02 / MANUAL COMPARISON

Time-consuming cross-checks

Dates, vehicle details, amounts and policy conditions must be compared carefully.

03 / IMAGE CONTEXT

Images need interpretation

A photograph may show damage, but cannot independently explain when or how it occurred.

04 / MISSING INFORMATION

Unclear evidence gaps

Claimants may not know what additional evidence is required or why it is needed.

05 / TRACEABILITY

Hard to follow reasoning

Reviewers need to connect findings to the source, calculation or policy clause.

06 / UNCERTAINTY

Risk of quick conclusions

A mismatch can have several explanations. It should trigger review, not automatic accusation.

03 · Proposed solution

One platform for submission, investigation and follow-up.

ClaimLens AI connects claimant and verifier activities with AI-assisted evidence understanding and rule-based checks.

CLAIMANT SIDE

Submit with clarity

  1. Create and save a claim draft.
  2. View a claim-specific evidence checklist.
  3. Capture or upload documents and photos.
  4. Submit the claim and track status.
  5. Respond to evidence requests and message the verifier.
VERIFIER SIDE

Review with context

  1. View claim queue and dashboard.
  2. Inspect original evidence.
  3. Run Gemini-assisted analysis.
  4. Review findings and compare sources.
  5. Request missing evidence, recalculate settlement and record a human decision.
How this solves the problem: it organizes evidence, highlights possible conflicts, explains what is missing and keeps review traceable. It assists the verifier; it does not independently approve or reject a claim.
04 · How the solution works

From raw evidence to review-ready information.

AI interpretation is kept separate from deterministic calculations and final human decisions.

01 · COLLECTClaim and evidenceIncident details, policy, photos, repair estimate and supporting files.
02 · UNDERSTANDGemini analysisExtract facts, observe image content and compare information across sources.
03 · VERIFYRule checksValidate dates, totals, vehicle details, limits, deductibles and required fields.
04 · INVESTIGATEFindings and requestsShow conflicts, uncertainty, alternative explanations and missing evidence.
05 · REVIEWHuman decisionVerifier reviews, recalculates where appropriate and records the decision.
05 · System architecture

How the components connect.

Architecture aligned with the technical specification: two React apps, one Node/Express API, MongoDB and backend-only Gemini integration.

USER INTERFACE
Claimant React App · Claim creation, checklist, uploads, status, messages and evidence responses
Verifier React App · Claim queue, dashboard, analysis, findings, requests and decisions
↓
BACKEND API
Node.js + Express REST API · Claim workflow · Evidence handling · API validation · Authentication and role authorization
↓
AI + RULE ENGINE
Gemini (backend only) · Extracts and compares facts from evidence
Deterministic rules · Validates calculations, dates, policy values and settlement arithmetic
↓
DATA & FILES
MongoDB + Mongoose · Claims, users, findings, requests, decisions and timeline
Evidence file storage · Original files retained; SHA-256 hash supports integrity checks
Important separation: Gemini assists with extraction and comparison. Deterministic backend code performs calculations and rule checks. The authorized verifier makes the claim decision.
06 · User flow

Two roles. One connected claim journey.

The claimant provides information, the verifier investigates, and both connect through evidence requests and responses.

A / CLAIMANT JOURNEY
  1. Sign in and start a new claim.
  2. Enter incident, vehicle, driver and policy details.
  3. Review the generated evidence checklist.
  4. Capture or upload photos and documents.
  5. Submit the claim for review.
  6. Track claim status and timeline.
  7. Respond to requests with files, a message or an unavailable reason.
B / VERIFIER JOURNEY
  1. Open the claim queue and select a claim.
  2. Take ownership and inspect original evidence.
  3. Run AI-assisted analysis.
  4. Review findings and source-comparison matrix.
  5. Request specific missing or unclear evidence.
  6. Review claimant response and rerun analysis if needed.
  7. Recalculate settlement and record the human decision.
Key handoffs: claim submission → verifier evidence request → claimant response. These handoffs connect both roles throughout the investigation.

08 · Example investigation

What if the evidence does not match?

A simple scenario to demonstrate the platform's reasoning approach.

CLAIMANT STATEMENT

“My vehicle was hit from behind.”

The claim narrative describes a rear collision.

UPLOADED PHOTO

Front-end damage appears visible

The submitted photograph appears to show damage around the front of the vehicle.

SYSTEM FINDING

Potential inconsistency identified

The statement and image appear to describe different damage locations. The finding links back to the statement and image used in comparison.

NEXT ACTION

Request clarification

Ask for additional vehicle photographs and the incident report. The verifier reviews the response before deciding.

Fairness safeguard: the system does not label this claim as fraud. Possible explanations include multiple impacts, an incomplete description or an incorrect image upload.

10 · Trust, safety and reliability

AI assistance with human accountability.

Responsible design matters because insurance decisions can have significant consequences.

Evidence traceability

Material findings point to supporting evidence or are marked as low-evidence hypotheses.

Reliable calculations

Dates, sums, limits, deductibles and settlement arithmetic use deterministic code.

Privacy and access

Claimant and verifier views are separated through server-side role-based authorization.

Graceful AI failure

If Gemini is unavailable, claim and evidence data remain intact; analysis can be retried.

Original evidence

Uploaded files are retained unchanged, with integrity signals such as SHA-256 hashes.

Human decision

AI does not approve, deny, accuse or decide fraud. An authorized verifier records the decision.
